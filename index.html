About

I am a Mathematics undergraduate at China Agricultural University, and my hometown is Yangzhou, Jiangsu. My work spans two directions: with Prof. Dallas Albritton, I study the anisotropic Kepler problem and its numerical simulation; with Prof. Feifan Zhang, I work on a dual-fractional vegetation-sand model, exploring pattern formation in arid ecosystems.
